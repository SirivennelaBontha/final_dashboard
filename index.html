Where did the profit go, and how much came back?

Before vs After comparison of the urban vertical farm. Before is Dataset 1 (Jan-Aug 2026, current state, no profit visibility). After is Dataset 2 (Jan-Aug 2027, simulated future state with the Farm Profit Hub app in use). 1,200 records each, same 28 variables.

1

The headline: more profit from the same farm

Same six crops, same two channels, same 1,200 records. Once costs were visible per crop and channel, gross profit rose for every crop.

Gross profit by crop

Variables: Crop_Name, Gross_Profit (sum). Grouped bar chart.

2

The surprise was in the margins

In 2026, Lettuce brought in the most revenue but kept the smallest share of it. The Pricing Tool showed break-even prices, so the weakest crops were repriced first.

Average gross margin by crop

Variables: Crop_Name, Gross_Margin_pct (mean). Horizontal bar chart.

Average selling price per kg

Variables: Crop_Name, Revenue / Sellable_Weight_kg. Horizontal bar chart.

3

Waste: produce that cost money but never sold

Harvests are now planned from open customer orders, and the Staff Portal flags waste above 6%. Less of each harvest is thrown away.

Average waste rate by crop

Variables: Crop_Name, Waste_Rate_pct (mean). Horizontal bar chart.

Total waste (kg) by crop

Variables: Crop_Name, Waste_kg (sum). Bar chart.

4

Cost per kg sold: where every dollar goes

Each bar splits the total cost of one sellable kg into its parts. "Other operating" is Total_Cost minus the six listed costs (water, facility and similar costs already inside Total_Cost in both datasets).

Cost per sellable kg by component

Variables: Electricity_Cost, Labor_Cost, Seed_Cost, Nutrient_Cost, Packaging_Cost, Distribution_Cost, Total_Cost, Sellable_Weight_kg. Stacked horizontal bar chart.

5

Channels: restaurants vs subscription boxes

Both channels gained. Delivery routes were grouped using order data, which cut distribution cost most for subscription boxes (many small home drops).

Revenue and gross profit by channel

Variables: Sales_Channel, Revenue, Gross_Profit (sum). Clustered column chart.

Gross margin by channel

Variables: Sales_Channel, Gross_Profit / Revenue. Column chart.

6

Month by month: steadier results

Same months compared year over year. The May 2026 dip came from fewer harvest batches that month (125 records against about 150 in other months). In 2027 harvests are planned from orders, so each month has the same number of batches.

Monthly gross profit

Variables: Month, Gross_Profit (sum). Line chart.
