Dataset note: Before = Dataset 1, 1,200 synthetic records (January–August 2026). After = Dataset 2, 1,200 simulated records (September 2026–April 2027) for the farm once the Farm Profit Hub is in use. Only two input variables were changed, each backed by research: waste kg −14.8% (order-based harvest planning; PCFWC, 2022) and labor hours −10% (digital task and record keeping; HortiDaily, 2025). Prices were not changed. KPIs are the average of each record's value, with the same formulas for both datasets: KPI 1 = gross profit ÷ revenue, KPI 2 = total cost ÷ sellable kg, KPI 3 = waste kg ÷ harvest kg, KPI 4 = sellable kg ÷ labor hours. Filters update every card and chart.
